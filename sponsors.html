Sponsor Binghamton CTF

Connect directly with top student cybersecurity engineers. Your support provides ChipWhisperer glitching kits, AI developer subscriptions, weekly meeting food, and student travel to MITRE eCTF.

What Your Sponsorship Funds

100% of sponsor contributions directly empower Binghamton students with hardware, compute tools, food, and competition access.

Hardware & Lab Gear

ChipWhisperer power analysis and voltage glitching kits, target microcontrollers (TI MSP430, STM32, NXP), and logic analyzers.

AI Tooling & Compute

Claude Pro, GitHub Copilot, and compute credits for automated firmware auditing, decompilation, and code analysis.

Hack Night Food

Weekly pizza, snacks, and soda sustaining 30+ students during intensive Thursday evening hack sessions in the Fab Lab.

Competition Travel

Gas, hotels, and registration sending student finalists to compete in person at MITRE headquarters in Bedford, MA.
