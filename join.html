Join Binghamton CTF

We teach cybersecurity from the ground up, with no prior experience needed. All majors and skill levels are welcome.

3 Easy Steps to Join

1

Join Discord

Connect with teammates, get meeting alerts, and ask questions directly to co-captains Samruddhi and Pratik.

2

Attend Meetings

Every Thursday at 5:00 PM in the Fab Lab (Engineering Building). Bring your laptop.

3

Practice & Compete

Solve beginner-friendly challenges on PicoCTF and TryHackMe, and join our spring MITRE eCTF team.

Join the CTF Discord Server

Scan the QR code with your phone camera or click the invite link to join our active community.

Helpful Courses & FAQ

CS 220: Computer Architecture

Assembly, registers, processor datapath, and memory hierarchies. Great for reverse engineering.

CS 310: Data Structures & Algorithms

Algorithmic complexity, memory representation, and pointer manipulation.

Do I need prior experience?

No! We teach everything from scratch, starting from basic Linux commands to binary reversing.

Is membership free?

Yes, 100% free for all Binghamton University students.
