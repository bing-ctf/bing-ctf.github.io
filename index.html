What We Practice

Hands-on disciplines covered in our weekly workshops and competitions.

Embedded & Hardware

Microcontroller firmware, UART/I2C sniffing, voltage glitching, and physical fault injection in MITRE eCTF.

MITRE Competition

Binary Exploitation

Disassemble x86/ARM binaries, audit C code, and craft exploits using Ghidra, GDB, and Pwntools.

Ghidra · Pwntools

Cryptography

Break insecure ciphers, analyze Diffie-Hellman key exchanges, and inspect cryptographic side channels.

RSA · AES · ECC

Web & Forensics

Expose web vulnerabilities (SQLi, SSRF, XSS), analyze PCAPs, and solve hands-on TryHackMe and PicoCTF challenges.

TryHackMe · PicoCTF
